### Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
 
An end-to-end data analytics project exploring customer demographics, membership tiers, and purchasing habits to optimize business revenue and satisfaction. 
 
### 🚀 Project Overview
 
This project processes transaction data from **348 customers** across 6 cities. By identifying regional friction points, demographic splits, and spending habits, it transforms raw data into actionable executive strategies. 
 
### 🧰 Technical Toolbox
 
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy):** Data cleaning, missing value handling, and snake_case column standardization.

* **PostgreSQL:** Relational database storage and advanced business intelligence querying.

* **Power BI:** Interactive executive dashboard visualizing regional KPIs and behavioral patterns.
 
### 📊 Core Analytics Insights
 
* **The Revenue Driver:** **Gold members** generate the highest revenue (**$153.4K**), beating Silver ($87.5K) and Bronze ($54K).

* **Extreme Demographics:** Membership profiles are highly regionalized; Bronze tiers in Chicago/Houston are **100% Female**, while Gold tiers in San Francisco are **100% Male**.

* **The Discount Fallacy:** **100% of unsatisfied customers** received a discount, showing that discounts are being used as reactive band-aids rather than loyalty builders.

* **Age vs. Value:** The **30-39 age group** drives the highest total volume ($192K+), but the **20-29 group** spends the most per capita (**$1,072.18 average spend**).

* **Satisfaction Gaps:** New York and San Francisco lead with top satisfaction scores (**3.00**), while Miami and Chicago drop to a critical **1.00**.
 
### 💡 Strategic Recommendations
 
1. **Fix Operations:** Stop using discounts reactively. Audit and fix the low-satisfaction service issues in Chicago and Miami.

2. **Targeted Campaigns:** Run higher-tier upsell marketing to females in the Midwest and premium positioning to males on the West Coast.

3. **Cohort Strategy:** Deploy high-volume product bundles for the 30-39 demographic and premium, elite offerings for the high-spending 20-29 segment.
